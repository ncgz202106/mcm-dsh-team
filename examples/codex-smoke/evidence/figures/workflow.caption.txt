Synthetic experiment and evidence flow. The model contract defines density-limited growth and constant per-capita loss. RK4 trajectories are evaluated against an analytic reference, including the zero-biomass and equal-rate cases. The nine scenarios use dt = 0.1. The baseline refinement uses dt = 0.2, 0.1 and 0.05.

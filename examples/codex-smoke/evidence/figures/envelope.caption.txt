Synthetic parameter envelope over all nine fixed scenarios. The shaded band is the pointwise minimum and maximum of the nine RK4 trajectories on the common 301-point grid (dt = 0.1). Dashed gray lines show every scenario, dotted blue boundaries delimit the band, and the solid blue line is the baseline. Growth values are 0.28, 0.35 and 0.42; mortality values are 0.04, 0.08 and 0.12; K = 100 and B0 = 12 throughout. The envelope represents these discrete parameter choices only. It is not a confidence interval, prediction interval, or estimate of real-world uncertainty.
